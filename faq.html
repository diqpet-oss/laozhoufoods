What is LaoZhou tofu?

The most useful facts about the food, sourcing position and available formats.

LaoZhou makes four Chinese tofu snack ranges: High-Broth Tofu, Classic Braised Tofu, Crispy-Skin Tofu and Meat-Style Tofu. Each range has a distinct texture and seasoning direction. Compare the tofu ranges.

The LaoZhou story began in 1986 in Yancheng, Jiangsu. The company entered a modern intelligent production base in Xinghua, Jiangsu, in 2024. Read the full company history.

The published range is presented as ambient, ready-to-eat tofu snacks. Always check the pack label for the selected product's storage, allergen and preparation instructions.

Braised tofu is typically pressed or fried for a firmer structure, then slowly cooked in a seasoned broth or sauce. That process gives it a denser, chewier texture and flavor that reaches beyond the surface. See LaoZhou's production process.

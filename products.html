The LaoZhou range

Four textures.
One deeply
savory craft.

From tender high-broth tofu to a crisp-skinned bite and firmer meat-style chew, every range starts with soy and is built around flavor that reaches through each piece.

LaoZhou’s tofu snacks are offered for international trade, foodservice and private-label review. Pack sizes and flavor directions are listed below; current ingredient, allergen and certification documents are confirmed for each selected SKU.

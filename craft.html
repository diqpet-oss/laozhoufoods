The process

Old craft.
Measured by
modern hands.

A traditional Chinese braise meets a connected production line. The tools are new, the respect for flavor and consistency is not.

The idea

Let the tofu
carry the flavor.

Good tofu starts with good soybeans and careful handling. A memorable braised tofu starts with patience. We select soybeans, control the soak, grind, form and fry, then give the tofu time to take on the character of the braise.

This is a process built around texture and depth: a surface sauce can be tasted immediately; a slow braise stays with the tofu.

From ingredient to texture

Three ideas behind
every satisfying bite.

The production line is precise. The final quality judgment is human.

01

Select the soy.

Carefully selected soybeans begin the process. Controlled soaking and grinding set the foundation for clean bean aroma and a smooth structure.

02

Build the bite.

Forming and dewatering are managed on a PLC-controlled line, helping create consistent tofu structure from batch to batch.

03

Give it time.

High-temperature frying creates the range’s characteristic texture. Slow braising lets broth and seasoning move through the tofu.

15

Standardized steps guide every piece of high-broth tofu, from ingredient handling to the sealed pack.

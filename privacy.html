Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

LaoZhou Foods respects the privacy of visitors and business contacts. This policy explains the information we may collect through laozhoufoods.com and how it is used.

Information we collect

When you submit an inquiry, we may collect your name, company, email address, phone number, country or market, business type, product interest and the content of your message. Basic technical information may also be processed by our website host to operate and protect the site.

How we use it

We use inquiry information to respond to your request, discuss products and samples, provide relevant business documentation and maintain appropriate business records. We do not sell contact details.

Sharing and retention

Information may be accessible to service providers that support website hosting, form delivery or business communications, and to relevant LaoZhou personnel and business partners where necessary to handle your inquiry. We retain information only as long as reasonably needed for these purposes or as required by law.

Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct or delete information you have provided, subject to applicable legal requirements. You can also ask not to be contacted further about a business inquiry. Email laozhoufood@gmail.com.

Third-party links

This website links to social media and other services. Their own privacy policies apply to information you provide on those platforms.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact LaoZhou Foods at laozhoufood@gmail.com.
